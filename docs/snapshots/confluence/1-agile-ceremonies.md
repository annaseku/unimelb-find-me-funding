## Standups and team meetings

- [Standup Summaries](standup-summaries.md): one entry per workshop, weeks 2 to 9, with tutor feedback; an activity record for weeks 10 to 12, reconstructed from Slack, GitHub and Jira; and the supervisor feedback given outside the workshops.
- [Standup Notes 14/09](standup-notes-1409.md): full notes for the Sprint 2 to 3 changeover.
- Working sessions arranged on Slack #all-student: frontend and backend integration (15/09), presentation planning and repository fixes (13/10), and presentation rehearsal with pre-demo testing (18/10).

Each standup answers the three questions from the Week 2 lecture: what did I do since the last meeting, what will I do before the next, and are there any blockers.

## Board and estimation

- [KAN Kanban board](https://annaseku01.atlassian.net/jira/software/projects/KAN/boards) is the source of truth for who is doing what (see the Sprint 3 page for the 21/09 decision to stay on Jira). A read-only copy is in the repository at [docs/snapshots/jira](https://github.com/annaseku/unimelb-find-me-funding/blob/main/docs/snapshots/jira/README.md).
- Story points use the Fibonacci scale (1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13). Draft estimates for each user story are on [User Stories and Acceptance Criteria](user-stories-and-acceptance-criteria.md); planning poker to confirm them is KAN-47.

## Continuous improvement

Each retrospective's action items are checked at the next one; the Sprint 3 retrospective shows which Sprint 2 actions were followed through and which slipped. The [Supervisor Feedback Log](supervisor-feedback-log.md) lists every piece of tutor and supervisor feedback, what we changed, and the evidence.
