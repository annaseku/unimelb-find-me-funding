## Description

**Find Me Funding** is a web tool that finds research funding for University of Melbourne researchers and ranks it by eligibility, research match, funding value and time to deadline. Team Crawlers, COMP30022 IT Capstone Project 2026, for the UoM Research Office (supervisor: Kerr Yuan).

[Live site](https://annaseku.github.io/unimelb-find-me-funding/) · [GitHub](https://github.com/annaseku/unimelb-find-me-funding) · [KAN board](https://annaseku01.atlassian.net/jira/software/projects/KAN/boards)
