# ADR-002 to ADR-004: Ranking, Normaliser and Deployment

> Snapshot of the Confluence page [ADR-002 to ADR-004: Ranking, Normaliser and Deployment](https://annaseku01.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/Crawlers/pages/46825514), taken 25 October 2026. The live page needs an Atlassian login. The client is referred to by role, not by name.

Summary of the three architecture decision records kept next to the code. Each ADR has the full context, options, consequences and evidence in the repository, which is the single source of truth. This page lets the decisions be found from Confluence too (KAN-44). Earlier decisions: [ADR-001: Technology Decisions](adr-001-technology-decisions.md) and [Crawler Architecture Decisions](crawler-architecture-decisions.md).

## How these were decided

Each followed the process on [4. Team Decision Making](4-team-decision-making.md): options and trade-offs written down, decided at a standup or sprint planning, then recorded. Anything that changes client-visible behaviour stays Proposed until the client confirms it. The open ranking questions are listed in [ranking discussion points](https://github.com/annaseku/unimelb-find-me-funding/blob/main/docs/07-requirements/ranking-discussion-points.md).
