## 1. What Went Well? 👍

What should we **continue doing**?

* Effective team communication and contribution to requirements analysis, modelling and architecture decisions
* Effective division of work between team members 
* Shared understanding of projects purpose, requirements, users and system structure

---

What caused difficulties, delays, confusion, or unnecessary work?

| Issue | Impact on Sprint |
| --- | --- |
| Delayed Project Start | Reduced the amount of time available for completing Sprint 1 activities. |

## 3. What Should We Improve? 💡

What could we do differently next sprint?

* Prioritise client communication to ensure requirements and assumptions can be validated before significant work begins
* Create documentation to track questions for the client for future meetings
* Continue collaborative approach to discussing requirements and architecture

What did we learn during this sprint?

# **Retrospective summary:**

  
Overall, the team worked effectively during Sprint 1 and successfully completed the majority of its planned activities. While the delayed project start and missed client meeting presented some challenges, they had limited impact on the team's overall progress. The primary improvement for the next sprint is to establish earlier and more consistent client communication so that requirements, assumptions, and architectural decisions can be validated before development progresses further.
